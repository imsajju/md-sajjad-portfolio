Story

Two years as Quality Head at a fast-paced startup.

At Valura.AI, an AI-native wealth platform moving at startup speed, I lead quality end to end — from intern who wrote the first hundred test cases to the Quality Head who decides whether a build is allowed near production.

The job spans Web, APIs, mobile, automation, performance, and security. Playwright and Maestro cut the manual grind. Semgrep, SonarQube, OWASP ZAP, Trivy, and GitLeaks sit in CI as real gates — triaged against OWASP Top 10 and CVE reality, not scanner theatre.

Fast teams don’t skip proof. They make proof keep up.

I also wear the CISO hat: risk assessments, compliance docs, audit prep — and an ISO 27001 certification that actually landed. Same discipline as testing: pipelines and paperwork both have to hold under pressure.

The goal is simple — turn quality from a late-stage checklist into the engineering discipline that ships confidence at the pace the product demands.

Method

How a build earns clearance.

A

Map risk first

KYC, EFT, portfolio, and agent paths get coverage weighted by blast radius — not by what’s easy to click.

B

Automate the boring

Playwright and Maestro hold regression. Humans spend time on judgment calls scanners can’t make.

C

Gate on security

SAST, DAST, SCA, and secrets fail the pipeline when findings are real — after CVE-aware triage.

D

Interrogate agents

Multi-turn behavior, output ranking, and production-path validation for AI systems that move money.

E

Leave an audit trail

ISO 27001-ready evidence: risks documented, controls testable, auditors not guessing.

F

Clear — or stop

Staging and prod verification with a binary outcome. No soft “seems fine.”
